What it does

Four checks, run on every scan

01

Claim isolation

Splits a response into individually checkable factual statements, so a false detail can't hide inside a true sentence.

02

Source cross-reference

Checks each claim against reference data and flags anything that doesn't match or can't be found.

03

Confidence scoring

Every claim gets a numeric score, not just a label — so you can decide your own threshold for what to trust.

04

Exportable reports

Save a scan's full breakdown to revisit later or attach as evidence in your own review process.

How a scan works

Three passes, same as any careful editor

1

Paste the text

Drop in a model's answer, a generated summary, or any passage you don't fully trust yet.

2

Claims get isolated

Each factual claim is pulled out on its own, so nothing hides inside a well-written sentence.

3

Each claim is scored

Verified, uncertain, or fabricated — with a confidence score and the reasoning behind it.
